# RI'S ART — scrub-scroll shot sheet

SEQUENCE: nail art studio — blush, crimson, gold, quiet luxury — 3 shots, 24s, ~420vh scroll

## Object inventory

| Axis | RI'S ART |
|---|---|
| Form | one relaxed feminine hand, long almond nails; a squat faceted glass polish bottle, gold cap |
| Material | high-gloss gel lacquer — one long specular streak, wet mirror sheen |
| Moving part | the polish brush, a bead of lacquer, flakes of gold leaf |
| Substance | viscous deep crimson lacquer (logo red), slow, self-levelling |
| Contact surface | blush-pink marble with faint gold veining |
| Signature detail | a single crimson bead sliding off the brush tip and levelling into a mirror-gloss nail — the brush that sits inside the "RI" monogram |

## OBJECT (byte-identical in every prompt)

> A relaxed feminine hand resting palm-down on blush-pink marble with faint gold veins,
> long almond-shaped nails, beside a small faceted glass nail-polish bottle filled with
> deep crimson lacquer and a brushed-gold cap. Soft single key light from above left,
> warm rim light, gentle haze, cream and blush tones with one crimson accent, subtle
> film grain. Shot on 85mm macro, shallow depth of field.

NEGATIVE: no text, no logos, no watermarks, no faces, no extra hands, no extra fingers,
no cuts, no zoom snaps, no camera shake, no motion blur smear.

### SHOT 1 — intrigue — hero "Art at your fingertips"
- START: OBJECT + `CAMERA: ECU on the polish brush held just above the bottle neck, one crimson bead forming at the brush tip, bottle filling the left third, hand soft and out of focus behind.`
- END: OBJECT + `CAMERA: ECU, slightly lower, the bead has dropped onto the tip of the index nail, first crimson gloss visible, marble veining in soft focus.`
- MOTION: `Slow continuous descent following the brush with a slight orbit right. The crimson bead stretches, releases and lands on the nail tip. Camera never stops.`
- TRANSITION OUT: continuous chain.

### SHOT 2 — reveal — "One stroke. Mirror gloss."
- START: Shot 1 END frame verbatim.
- END: OBJECT + `CAMERA: MCU, the whole hand in the left half, all five nails glossy crimson, one fine gold-leaf line on the ring finger, lens just above the marble.`
- MOTION: `Continuous pull back and slow crane down while orbiting left. The brush sweeps one stroke, lacquer levels to a mirror shine, a flake of gold leaf settles on the ring nail. No cuts.`
- TRANSITION OUT: continuous chain.

### SHOT 3 — rest — "Book your chair"
- START: Shot 2 END frame verbatim.
- END: OBJECT + `CAMERA: MS, hand and bottle small in the left third, wide empty blush marble on the right, horizon of the marble at one third.`
- MOTION: `Slow decelerating pull back and rise. Light glides across the glossy nails in one long highlight. Motion comes to rest.`

Why not generic: the brush-drop is the monogram's own brush; the lacquer *levels* (state change),
gold leaf lands (state change), camera cranes on Y. Empty right half reserved for headlines.
